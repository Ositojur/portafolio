How I work

From a real need to a solution the team can use.

  1. Understand the need How you operate today, what repeats, and who decides.
  2. Design the solution A simple path, with a scope that can actually be delivered.
  3. Build and test Development and testing on a phone and a computer, with you reviewing it.
  4. Hand off and document It goes live, with an explanation of how to use it.

What we agree before building

A first stage usually includes

  • One main path the client or the team can complete.
  • Testing on a phone and a computer.
  • One round of corrections on what was agreed.
  • An explanation of how to use it.

We write it down

  • What is in this stage, and what is not.
  • What the business needs to provide (copy, photos, a product list).
  • The timeline once those materials are in.
  • A new feature is quoted before it is built.

The price comes from the scope, not from a generic list. In this meeting the goal is to understand the problem and propose a first step that can be paid for and delivered.

Questions that usually come up

Do you only build websites, or systems too?

Both. A clear website, a catalog, or an internal flow for orders and requests, depending on what the business needs first.

Can I see more projects?

Yes. The portfolio has the rest of the cases. In the meeting it is better to open just one, the closest to your problem.

What if we also need a brand?

There is a separate identity proposal. If it fits, we look at it after the digital problem is defined, not at the same time.

Next step

If the problem is clear, the next message is a one-page proposal: what gets solved, what is included, and the price. If a detail is missing, we ask for it before quoting.
