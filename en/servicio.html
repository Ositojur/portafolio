What we agree on before building

A first phase usually includes

  • One main flow that your customer or team can complete.
  • Testing on phone and desktop.
  • One round of fixes on what we agreed.
  • A walkthrough of how to use it.

We put it in writing

  • What's in and what's out of this phase.
  • What the business needs to provide: text, photos, product list.
  • The timeline, which starts once those materials are in.
  • Any new feature gets quoted before it's built.

The store has reference prices for all nine services. The final number comes from the scope: in this meeting we pin down the problem and pick a first step you can afford and I can deliver. To start, you pay a deposit; the balance is due on delivery.
