I build systems. Then I try to break them.

Everything I deliver goes through one question: how would someone attack this? And if you already have a website or a system, I review it the same way.

I review companies the way someone trying to get in would.

I have a degree in cybersecurity and specialize in cybercrime. With signed authorization, I test a company's websites, servers and networks to find the flaws before an attacker does. Then we fix them together. And if the system is old, I modernize it in stages without stopping day-to-day work.

Penetration testing
Controlled attacks on websites, networks and applications, with the scope agreed in writing.
Hardening
Permissions, encryption, two-factor authentication, backups and monitoring on servers and dashboards.
Modernization
I move legacy systems to a current stack, piece by piece and without losing data.
Incidents
If something already happened, I investigate what occurred and preserve the digital evidence.
People
Phishing drills and talks so the team becomes a barrier too.
