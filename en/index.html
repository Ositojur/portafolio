About

I’m Lautaro Demian Núñez, a developer with a cybersecurity degree, based in Córdoba, Argentina. For more than six years I have built digital tools for companies, shops, and small businesses.

I start by finding where the friction is: a process that takes too long, tools that do not talk to each other, or a website that makes the experience harder. From there I design and build a solution that fits.

How I can help

  • WebsitesPresent your business and make it easy for clients to get in touch.
  • Shops and catalogsShow your products and help people find what they want.
  • Custom systemsKeep orders, bookings, and information in one place.
  • ImprovementsSimplify the repetitive steps that waste time today.

Process

From a real need to something your team can use.

  1. Understand the need Learn the business, its processes, and the problem to solve.
  2. Design the solution Organize the information and define a simple experience.
  3. Build and test Develop it and check that it works comfortably on a phone and a computer.
  4. Hand off and document Put it into use and explain how to run it.

Your next project starts with a conversation.

A website that represents your business better. A task that stops eating your time. Tell me what you want to improve and we will find a way to do it.
