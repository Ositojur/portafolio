So the offer and the evidence stay separate

The landings describe what can be built. The portfolio shows what already exists. Happy Ride is a booking request with confirmation by the business. Do not present it as automatic payment or as a hotel booking engine.

Payments, AI assistants, channel managers, and the identity package are commercial offers. If someone asks “have you already built it that way?”, show the closest case and explain what would be added for their project.
