Questions about Identity

Can I use the identity in any medium?

Yes. It is ready for digital and print: web, social, stationery, and presentations, with usage rules in the brand book.

What if I want changes after the 2 rounds?

The two rounds cover adjustments to what was delivered. A change of direction, or new pieces, is quoted separately before any work starts.

Can the brand book be updated later?

Yes. It is a living document. When the brand grows, it can be updated without starting from zero.

Can I add the website later?

Yes. The identity stays as the base. The site or the system is agreed as a next stage, with its own scope.
